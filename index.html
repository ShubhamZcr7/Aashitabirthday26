A Note From Me

Today I celebrate you — not only because the calendar tells me to, but because every day with you teaches me what gentle courage, stubborn kindness and contagious laughter look like. You fill ordinary moments with color: the quiet mornings we share coffee, the way you hum when you think I'm not listening, the small, brave choices you make that show what a beautiful person you are.

I don't have grand words that change the world, only the small promise that I will try to be the person who sees you clearly, supports your quiet ambitions, and keeps making space for your light. If life is a story, you are the chapters I re-read — the parts I am still discovering and eager to keep exploring. You deserve soft comforts, loud celebrations, and every dream coming true.

Keep being you — curious, fierce, tender. Thank you for the memory of a hand in mine, for patience when I'm clumsy, and for every laugh that made our days brighter. Happy birthday, love — here's to many more years of simple magic, shared glances, and the quiet certainty that we belong to each other.
